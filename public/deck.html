Product — Live Today

Everything a merchant needs.
Nothing they don't.

  • Business registration with profile and social media links stored onchain
  • Payment links — fixed or open amount, single or multi-use
  • Invoices with line items, quantities, VAT, and due date
  • QR code generation for every payment link and invoice
  • Transaction history with onchain proof (block explorer link)
  • Circle Passkey wallet — no seed phrase, login with fingerprint
  • MetaMask and Rabby support via ConnectKit
  • Arc Testnet and Arc Mainnet — dual chain support

Why Arc blockchain?

  • USDC is the native gas token — no ETH needed
  • Sub-second finality — payments confirm instantly
  • Predictable, stable transaction fees
  • Built by Circle — same team behind USDC
